⚠️ Use AI Responsibly

AI can be a powerful learning assistant, but it should support your learning rather than replace it.

  • ✅ Verify important information.
  • ✅ Understand the answer instead of copying it.
  • ✅ Use AI to practice and explore.
  • ❌ Do not blindly trust generated information.
  • ❌ Do not submit AI-generated work without understanding it.
